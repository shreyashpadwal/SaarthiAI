About Saarthi AI

Saarthi AI is an open-source autonomous multi-agent research system. Multiple AI agents collaborate — planning, searching the web, reading sources, summarizing, and writing — to produce comprehensive research reports on any topic. All processing runs locally on your machine.

❓ FAQ
Common questions
Is my data private? ▼
Yes. The server runs on localhost. Only your API calls to Groq and Tavily leave your machine — no other third party sees your data.
Why does research take 5-10 minutes? ▼
The agent searches multiple sources, reads each one, summarises, compresses, and writes a full report — all sequentially (concurrent=1) to stay within Groq's free-tier rate limits.
What if I get a rate limit error? ▼
Wait 2-3 minutes and try again. The Groq free tier has token-per-minute limits. Keep Concurrent=1 and use gpt-oss-120b for both models.
How does PDF export work? ▼
The PDF is generated entirely in your browser using jsPDF. It includes a gradient cover page, formatted sections with colour-coded headings, tables, bullet lists, and a branded footer. No server needed.
